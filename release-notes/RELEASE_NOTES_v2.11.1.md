# TeacherPro v2.11.1

A small layout follow-up to 2.11.0.

## Changed

- The weekly planner now uses more of the available window width: its maximum
  content width is **1800px** instead of 1600px. Large displays no longer show
  wide empty gutters on either side of the week.

No vault, file-format or behavior changes.

## Downloads

- **macOS**: `.dmg` (universal)
- **Windows**: `.msi` or `.exe` installer
- **Linux**: `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage`

> Release builds are unsigned. On macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open**
> the first time you launch it; on Windows, SmartScreen may ask you to confirm.
